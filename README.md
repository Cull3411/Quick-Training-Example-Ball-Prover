# Quick Training Example: Ball Prover and Meter Factor

Personal training sample by **Todd Davis** ([Cull3411](https://github.com/Cull3411)).

This deck was made to present at an interview for a company that sold devices that measured liquid flows for the energy pipeline industry. It is an example of training style: a simple visual aid for walking someone through pipe proving and meter factors, not a finished product for customer consumption.

Companion sample: [Quick-Training-Example-Analog-to-Digital](https://github.com/Cull3411/Quick-Training-Example-Analog-to-Digital).

## What the visual covers

- Why custody-transfer measurement has to be proved: paying suppliers, billing buyers, transportation charges, and regulatory requirements
- A simplified pipe-prover flow: flow meter, prover section, temperature and pressure, prover pulse counter, flow computer, transfer line
- A bi-directional ball prover layout
- Meter factor as the correction between the flow meter and the prover: (Meter Factor) x (Flow Meter Measurement) = Prover Measurement
- Further-study pointers from the deck: K-factor, measurement uncertainty, repeatability, piston provers, unidirectional provers, bidirectional provers
